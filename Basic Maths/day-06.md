# Basic Math Concepts

1. **Euclidean Theorem -**
<br>
<br>
Consider 2 numbers - N1, N2 (N1 > N2)
<br>
GCD(N1, N2) = GCD(N1-N2, N2)
<br>
<br>
*Ex. - GCD(20, 15) = GCD (5, 15) ------ 20-15 = 5
<br>
<br>
GCD(15, 5) = GCD(10, 5) ------15-5 = 10
<br>
<br>
GCD(10, 5) = GCD(5, 5) ------10-5 = 5
<br>
<br>
GCD(5, 5) = GCD(0, 5) ------5-5 = 0
<br>
<br>
Therefore, 5 is the GCD<br>
Better way - <br>
GCD(N1, N2) = GCD(N1%N2, N2)*<br>
<br>
**If greater%smaller = 0 , other one is GCD**
